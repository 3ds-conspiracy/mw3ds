# Contributing

Bug reports and suggestions are welcome as **GitHub issues**. **Pull requests are not accepted** and will be closed
without review, however small.

## Filing an issue

Include what helps reproduce it:

- What you did, what you expected, and what happened instead.
- Where in the game (cell name, quest, NPC) and whether it was on a real New 3DS or in Azahar.
- The build: the commit hash and how you built it (`.3dsx` or CIA).
- The log, `sdmc:/3ds/mw3ds/log.txt` (in Azahar: `<azahar>\user\sdmc\3ds\mw3ds\log.txt`), and a screenshot if it is visual.
- For a test failure: the test name and its `RESULT:` line.

## Please do not

- Attach or link Morrowind game files, converted data, `.3dsx` / `.cia` builds, or screenshots of large amounts of game
  content. This project does not distribute any of it.
- Post your `.env` or paths that identify you.
