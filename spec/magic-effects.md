# Magic effects: what each one does when applied

Taken from OpenMW's `apps/openmw/mwmechanics/`: `spelleffects.cpp` (`applyMagicEffect`, `applyActorMagicEffect`,
`removeMagicEffect`, `applyProtections`), `activespells.cpp` (the list of active spells, stacking, expiry),
`spellcasting.cpp` (`CastSpell::inflict`, `cast`), `spellresistance.cpp`, `magiceffects.cpp` (the per-actor totals),
`summoning.cpp`, and the per-frame bits of `actors.cpp`. In our words; ids are the ESM effect indices this repo uses
(`out/world/game.json`, 0..136). The cost, cast chance, resistance roll and Reflect / Absorption odds are in
[magic.md](magic.md); this page is about what an effect does once it lands.

Test: `openmw-spec-effects` (generated by `tools/specgen_effects.py`). What the engine cannot report yet:
[findings/magic-effects.md](findings/magic-effects.md).

## 1. The life of an effect

**Casting** (`CastSpell::cast`, `inflict`). A spell, scroll, potion, ingredient or enchantment hands each of its
effects, by range (self / touch / target), to the target as one *active spell* (`ActiveSpells`). The caster's own
self-range effects land on the caster; touch and target ones on what is hit. An area effect hits every actor inside the
radius except the caster and the one already hit. An effect flagged caster-linked (the Absorb family) is dropped when
the caster is not an actor. When every effect of the cast is non-recastable (bound items) and that spell is still
active on the target, the cast does nothing ("sMagicCannotRecast").

**One active spell per source.** Active spells are keyed by (spell or item id, caster, item). Casting the same
spell at the same target again does not stack: the old copy's effects are marked finished and the new copy applies in
its place (`ActiveSpells::addToSpells`; merging in is allowed only when none of the new effects shares an effect index
with the old, which for a repeat cast of one spell never holds). Potions and ingredients are flagged stackable: each
drink is its own entry. Different spells always stack, and so do two effects of one spell (separate effect indices).

**Applying** (`applyMagicEffect`), once per frame for every effect of every active spell, with `dt` the frame time:
1. First frame: roll the magnitude, run protections (3), then the effect's own handler (section 4).
2. An effect that is one-shot per cast (flag "applied once", see 2) is done after that; it only counts its time down.
3. Otherwise it is re-run every frame (per-second effects).
4. The effect's totals entry (`MagicEffects`, keyed by effect and by skill / attribute) gets `+magnitude` when it
   first lands and is adjusted if the magnitude changes; the total is the sum of all active copies, truncated to
   1/1024. Everything that "reads" an effect (armor, sneak, combat, movement, casting) reads that total.
5. Time left falls by `dt`. At 0 the effect is erased on the next update and its **removal handler** runs (section 4),
   after the total drops by the magnitude. An effect with no duration (0) has time 0 (set by the caller, outside the files read): it applies on its first frame
   and is erased on the next update.
   A duration of -1 is a permanent effect (abilities, constant enchantments, diseases): never erased by time.

**Magnitude** is `min + uniform(0 .. max - min)` as an integer; with min equal to max it is that. It is rolled once, at
the first frame. Duration in the spell record is in seconds. (Where the duration is clamped to at least 1 for effects
that are not applied-once happens in the Lua-side caller, `mwlua`, outside this reading list: spec/magic.md has the
cost-side rule.)

## 2. Per-second or one-shot, and why it matters

For a non-"no magnitude", non-"applied once" effect with a duration above 0, the magnitude each frame is
`magnitude x min(time left, dt)`: a total of exactly magnitude x duration over the life, spread over the frames, the last
one cut short. With duration 0 the whole magnitude lands in one frame. This is the rule for:

- Fire / Frost / Shock Damage, Poison, Damage Health / Magicka / Fatigue, Damage Attribute, Damage Skill (a fraction
  of a point per frame accumulates on the stat), Sun Damage, Disintegrate Weapon / Armor (the fractional remainder is
  kept on the item), Restore Health / Magicka / Fatigue / Attribute / Skill, Absorb Health / Magicka / Fatigue.

The "applied once" effects are the reversible ones: the handler runs on the first frame, the same amount is undone by
the removal handler at the end. By the pairing of apply and undo in `spelleffects.cpp` these are Drain and Fortify of
every kind, Absorb Attribute / Skill, Fortify Maximum Magicka, Fortify Attack, the summons and bound items, the AI
effects (Calm, Frenzy, Demoralize, Rally, Turn Undead), Command, and every modifier-only effect (section 5). The ESM bit
for it (0x1000) is not set on any effect in this repo's converted data, so a table of which effects are
applied-once has to be derived from the code like this, not from the flag.

A Drain or Fortify (reversible) cast with duration 0 therefore lands and is undone at the next update: no lasting effect.

Dynamic-stat helper: `adjustDynamicStat` adds to the *current* value, clamped to 0 and to the maximum (modified)
value, unless told otherwise. Drain passes "may go below 0" (a drained pool can be negative, and Drain Health can kill);
Fortify passes "may exceed the maximum".

## 3. Protections: godmode, Reflect, Absorption, resistance

`applyProtections` runs on an effect's first frame (not for abilities and the like, not on effects already applied):

1. **Reflect / Spell Absorption** only for a spell-type source (flag temporary: spells, potions, scroll enchantments, not
   constant effects) and only when target is not the caster. If the target has Reflect > 0, the effect is not
   unreflectable and the caster exists: for each *applied* Reflect effect on the target, roll 0..99; below its magnitude
   means the effect is reflected back (re-cast with the target as the new caster; resistance then applies to the
   original caster). Else, if the target has Spell Absorption > 0: the same roll per Absorption effect; on success the
   effect is removed and the target's current magicka rises by the spell's cost (not clamped to the maximum), the
   enchantment cast cost for an item. Each applied Reflect or Absorption entry on the target gets its own roll, in the order the target's active effects are listed, and the first success ends the checks.
2. A harmful effect that lands on an actor other than the caster counts as a hit: the actor reacts (combat).
3. **Resistance**: unless the effect is flagged "ignore resistances" (abilities, diseases and curses carry it), the
   multiplier `1 - resistance/100` from `getEffectResistance` (magic.md) scales the minimum and the maximum magnitude.
   A multiplier of 0 removes the effect with the "resisted" message. Effects flagged no-magnitude take no scaling here:
   their resistance roll (full or none) already decided. So resistance is rolled **once**, at the first frame, and a
   per-second effect keeps that scaling for its whole life. A multiplier above 1 (Weakness) is allowed and raises the
   magnitude. Self-cast effects are resisted like any other: the roll, not the caster, matters.

God mode on the player (a debug mode) skips Drain, Damage, Absorb, Disintegrate; Drain / Absorb effects are even dropped.
The part of the roll that does not depend on the target's resist effects is below 1 percent of the magnitude whenever
the caster is not unusually bad at the spell (for `x > roll` the resisted amount is `roll / min(x, 100)`, below 1).

## 4. What each effect does

"Total" means it only adds to the effect's entry in the actor's magnitudes; the systems named read it (those readers
are outside the files this page reads, from general OpenMW knowledge; a name is a pointer to check, not a quote).
Resist column: the effect that resists it (R = Resist Magicka, W = Weakness to Magicka) from `getResistanceEffect`;
"none" = never resisted.

### 4.1 Dynamic stats

| Id | Effect | Kind | Rule | Ends | Resist |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 14, 15, 16 | Fire / Shock / Frost Damage | per-second | current health falls by the amount (clamped at 0, not below); counts as magical damage (hit overlay, kill credit) | nothing to undo | Resist Fire / Shock / Frost, less Weakness to the same, plus the matching Fire / Lightning / Frost **Shield** counts as resistance |
| 27 | Poison | per-second | as Damage Health | none | Resist Poison less Weakness to Poison |
| 23 | Damage Health | per-second | as above; on an ability (base-value spell) it lowers the *maximum* instead | none | R / W |
| 24, 25 | Damage Magicka / Fatigue | per-second | current falls, clamped at 0 (fatigue below 0 only with the "uncapped damage fatigue" setting) | none | R / W |
| 135 | Sun Damage | per-second | exterior (or quasi-exterior) only: amount x clamp(max(sun visibility x sun height, fMagicSunBlockedMult x sun height), 0, 1) | none | none |
| 18, 19, 20 | Drain Health / Magicka / Fatigue | one-shot, reversible | current value falls by the magnitude, **may go negative**; the maximum is untouched; magicka and fatigue the same | the same amount is added back (no clamp to 0, clamped to the maximum) | R / W |
| 80, 81, 82 | Fortify Health / Magicka / Fatigue | one-shot, reversible | current value rises by the magnitude, **may exceed** the old maximum, and the maximum rises with it (on an ability the base value is raised instead; that the maximum follows the total is read in code outside this page's files) | current falls by the amount, **may go below 0**, so damage taken meanwhile is not forgiven | none |
| 84 | Fortify Maximum Magicka | one-shot | the magicka maximum is recomputed: `int((fPCbaseMagickaMult + 0.1 x total) x Intelligence)` (the player; NPCs use fNPCbaseMagickaMult); current scales with it (creaturestats.cpp, not read here) | recompute | none |
| 136 | Stunted Magicka | total | read by rest (below) and the magicka maximum; no stat change itself | none | none |
| 75, 76, 77 | Restore Health / Magicka / Fatigue | per-second | current rises, clamped at the maximum | none | none |
| 86, 87, 88 | Absorb Health / Magicka / Fatigue | per-second | target current falls (clamped at 0) and the **caster gains the full amount** (clamped at the caster's maximum) even when the target had less | none | R / W |

Rest (`actors.cpp`): with Stunted Magicka on, sleeping restores magicka only for the hours after the effect has run
out (a permanent one: none). Drowning: no Water Breathing and submerged past the held breath: fSuffocationDamage per
second (3), not in god mode.

### 4.2 Attributes and skills

Attribute / skill value = base + modifier - damage, floored at 0 (an attribute's `damage` accumulator; skills the same).

| Id | Effect | Rule | Ends | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 17, 21 | Drain Attribute / Skill | adds `magnitude` to the damage accumulator, **no cap** | restores that much (never above full) | NPCs only for skills (a creature has none: the effect is dropped); R / W |
| 22, 26 | Damage Attribute / Skill | per-second; adds to the accumulator, but each tick's amount is capped at the current modified value | **lasting**: nothing undoes it | the only way back is Restore (or the stat's natural recovery elsewhere); skills on NPCs only; R / W |
| 74, 78 | Restore Attribute / Skill | per-second; subtracts from the accumulator, never below 0: **cannot lift a stat above its base** | none | skill: NPCs only; none |
| 79, 83 | Fortify Attribute / Skill | adds to the modifier; on an ability, to the base | the same amount removed | not resisted; skill: NPCs only |
| 85, 89 | Absorb Attribute / Skill | the target gets the damage of Drain-like kind, the **caster the same amount as Fortify**, for the duration | target restored, caster fortify removed | target skill only if NPC, caster skill only if NPC; R / W; a self-cast Absorb nets 0 |

A Drain and a Fortify on the same stat net: the modifier and the accumulator are separate. Casting the same spell again
does not double any of this (section 1). There is no cap on the modifier in the code read here (a skill or attribute
above 100 is possible while fortified).

### 4.3 Resist, weakness, elemental shields

Resist Fire / Frost / Shock (90, 91, 92), Resist Magicka (93), Resist Common / Blight / Corprus Disease (94, 95, 96),
Resist Poison (97), Resist Normal Weapons (98), Resist Paralysis (99), Weakness to Fire / Frost / Shock / Magicka /
Common / Blight / Corprus / Poison / Normal Weapons (28..36), Fire / Lightning / Frost Shield (4, 5, 6): **totals only**.
They are read by `getEffectResistance` (the table in magic.md), the weapon-hit code (normal weapons) and the disease
code. Resist and Weakness may exceed 100 and stack linearly across spells; the multiplier is floored at 0 at the end
only. The Weakness effects themselves are resisted by Resist Magicka. Shield (3) is read by armor rating; the three
elemental shields also act as resistance to their element (section 4.1) and burn the attacker (combat code).

### 4.4 Cure, remove, dispel

| Id | Effect | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| 69, 70 | Cure Common / Blight Disease | removes the target's spells of type disease (Common) or blight (Blight) from its spell list and from its active spells (`purgePermanent`) |
| 100 | Remove Curse | the same for curses |
| 71 | Cure Corprus Disease | removes **active effects of Corprus only** (the Corprus effect and its worsening effects are cleared); the disease spell stays on the list |
| 72 | Cure Poison | removes all *applied* Poison effects from every active spell |
| 73 | Cure Paralyzation | the same for Paralyze |
| 57 | Dispel | for each active spell that is a temporary one and of spell type (not potions, enchantments, abilities, powers), one roll 0..99 below the magnitude removes the **whole spell** (all of its effects, their undo handlers run). Magnitude 100 or more removes all of them. A Dispel cast as a spell is itself one of the spells it may remove |

None of these is resisted. An effect removed by purge runs its removal handler, so Drain / Fortify / bound items go
away exactly as when they time out.

### 4.5 Fortify Attack, modifiers read elsewhere, senses

These add to the effect total and nothing else happens in `spelleffects.cpp`. Where each is read (general OpenMW
knowledge, outside the reading list; to verify when a test is written): Shield (3) armor rating; Burden (7) / Feather (8)
carrying capacity; Jump (9) jump height; Levitate (10) allowed only if levitation is enabled (else removed with
a message); Slowfall (11) fall damage; Swift Swim (1) swim speed; Water Walking (2) see below; Water Breathing (0) stops
drowning (read in `actors.cpp`: no damage while its magnitude is > 0); Chameleon (40) and Invisibility (39) hit chance
and detection; Light (41) the light on the actor; Sanctuary (42) the chance to be hit; Night Eye (43) brightness (a
negative total is clamped away at the end of the effect, see its handler); Fortify Attack (117) the player's attack
roll; Blind (47) the actor's own hit chance; Sound (48) the spell-cast chance (magic.md); Silence (46) forbids casting; Paralyze (45) freezes the actor; Charm (44) raises disposition;
Telekinesis (59) interaction range; Detect Animal / Enchantment / Key (64, 65, 66) the detection overlay;
Spell Absorption (67) and Reflect (68) the protection roll above; Soul Trap (58) below; Vampirism (133) and Corprus
(132) below.

- **Water Walking** (2): refused (effect removed with "sMagicInvalidEffect") if the target cannot be put on water at the
  moment of the first frame; removed at once for a pure water creature already swimming.
- **Soul Trap** (58): see 4.7.
- **Sound** (48): on the player a looping "magic sound" is started at volume (total + magnitude) / 100 clamped to 0..1,
  stopped when the total reaches 0.
- **Night Eye** (43): at the end, if the total is below 0 and its base below 0, the base is moved toward 0 by the
  smaller of the two (a bug workaround for brightness clamping).

### 4.6 AI effects

All of these change the target's AI setting by the magnitude and undo it. Creature and humanoid variants are separate
effects: the wrong kind of target (humanoid effect on a creature, creature on a humanoid) is **invalid** and the effect
is dropped; the player is never affected (Frenzy, Calm, Demoralize, Rally).

| Id | Effect | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| 51, 52 | Frenzy Humanoid / Creature | Fight rating + magnitude |
| 49, 50 | Calm Humanoid / Creature | Fight rating - magnitude; with magnitude > 0 the target stops fighting now. With the "classic calm spells" setting, any calm > 0 re-stops combat every frame |
| 53, 54 | Demoralize | Flee rating + magnitude |
| 55, 56 | Rally | Flee rating - magnitude |
| 101 | Turn Undead | Flee rating + magnitude; invalid on NPCs and non-undead creatures |
| 44 | Charm | invalid on non-NPCs; otherwise just a total (disposition code reads it) |
| 118, 119 | Command Creature / Humanoid | only an actor caster; never the player; wrong kind invalid; applies only if the magnitude is **at least the target's level**: a "commanded" follow package is stacked on it; the package is removed when the total reaches 0. The per-frame `isCommanded` check also requires magnitude >= level |

All of them are resisted by Resist Magicka (R / W) except Command (not listed in the resist map).

### 4.7 Summons, bound items, soul trap, disintegrate, lock and open, travel

- **Summons** (102..116, 134; the creature id comes from a game setting per effect, e.g. `sMagicScampID`): the creature
  is placed near the summoner (120 units), made to follow and help it, and recorded under the effect. If the placement
  fails the effect stays active with no creature. When the effect ends the creature is removed. While active, if the
  summoned creature dies (and its death animation is over) the summoning effect is **purged**, so a new one can be
  cast. Not resisted. Only in a loaded cell. (Wolf, Bear, Bonewolf and the two spare creature summons exist in OpenMW's
  list; this repo's data has none of them.)
- **Bound items** (120..125, 127..131; the item id from a game setting, e.g. `sMagicBoundDaggerID`; Bound Gloves adds both
  gauntlets): the item is added and equipped; the weapon slot also draws the weapon. If it cannot be equipped (beast
  races: boots, helm) the effect ends at once. At the end the item is removed, and on the player the previously equipped
  item is re-equipped when the bound one was in the slot. Non-recastable. Died actor: all effects are cleared, so the item
  goes.
- **Extra Spell** (126): on an NPC, unequips everything except weapon, ammunition, pants, and (if not armor) the left
  hand; autoequip again when it ends. The player is not unequipped.
- **Soul Trap** (58): on the death of a creature with a soul value > 0 that has Soul Trap > 0 with a *caster who is an
  actor*, the caster's smallest empty soul gem that is large enough (gem value x fSoulgemMult) takes the soul. One gem of a
  stack only. A creature with soul 0 gives the player "sMagicInvalidTarget" on cast.
- **Disintegrate Armor** (38): the first worn piece in the order shield, cuirass, left pauldron, right pauldron, left
  gauntlet, right gauntlet, helmet, greaves, boots that has condition left loses magnitude (per-second, fractions kept) and
  stops there; at 0 the item unequips (the player) or a replacement is chosen (an NPC). **Disintegrate Weapon** (37): the
  right-hand item. Not on targets without an inventory (invalid). Not in god mode.
- **Lock** (12): on a lockable object: lock level becomes the rolled magnitude if the current lock level is lower (a
  stronger lock is untouched); on anything else the effect is invalid. **Open** (13): also an unlock attempt for the
  crime code (made as the *player* regardless of the caster); unlocks if lock level <= magnitude, else the fail sound.
  Neither is resisted.
- **Mark** (60) stores the player's cell and position; **Recall** (61) teleports the player there if a mark exists;
  **Divine / Almsivi Intervention** (62, 63) teleport to the closest divine / temple marker. Only for the player (invalid on
  anyone else); refused with "sTeleportDisabled" when teleporting is off. The effect acts on the first frame only and is
  then removed. Recall, the interventions and Invisibility are not applied in a zero-time frame (a paused menu), so a teleport never
  happens while stat screens update. Not resisted.
- **Invisibility** (39): equipping a constant-effect item purges it (`ActiveSpells::update`); breaking it on attack or cast
  is in other files.
- **Telekinesis / Detect** (59, 64..66): total only; the overlays are the renderer's.
- **Vampirism** (133), **Corprus** (132): Vampirism total survives death (it is re-added after the effects are cleared);
  Corprus: the first frame starts a worsening clock, every worsening period re-applies the spell's other applied-once effects
  (the *corprus effects*) and shows "sMagicCorprusWorsens"; when the Corprus effect is removed the
  harmful ones are undone once per worsening and the active spell is cleared but its params kept. Cure Corprus purges Corprus only.

### 4.8 Who gets credit, and death

A health-damaging effect (Fire, Shock, Frost, Poison, Sun Damage, Damage Health, Absorb Health) that kills an actor counts
as a kill by its caster if that is the player or one of the player's followers (reputation and bounty code then runs).
A werewolf caster gets a kill count. When an actor's death animation ends, all its active spells are cleared (so bound
items and summons go), with Vampirism kept.

## 6. What our tests rely on

`openmw-spec-effects` uses only effects whose result is visible with the EXPECT kinds that exist (`attr`, `skill`,
`health`, `healthmax`, `magicka`, `magickamax`, `effect`, `armor`, `refhealth`, `refmagicka`, `refcombat`, `refflee`):

- drain / fortify attribute and skill, with the floor at 0, the independent expiry of two spells and the "same spell
  does not stack" rule;
- damage and restore attribute and skill (instant, stays; never above base), per-second damage totals;
- health: per-second damage and poison, Drain Health (maximum unchanged, given back), Fortify Health (maximum and
  current, no protection), instant damage then restore, restore per second capped by the loss;
- magicka: damage, restore, drain, fortify, Fortify Maximum Magicka;
- resist / weakness by element on the player (via `ACTIVE` effects) and on a scamp (its own resists), absorb on a scamp,
  cure poison, dispel, the totals of modifier-only effects (shield, burden, feather, chameleon, sanctuary, resists...), the
  wrong-kind AI effects on a creature.

Tolerances: a resistable magnitude may be up to 1 percent lower (section 3), plus half a point of rounding; per-second
effects a point and a half either way. The random part of the resist roll needs the player's Willpower and Luck low
(5 here) so the part is as small as it can be.
